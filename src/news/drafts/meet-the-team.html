From editing hype reels for the H1Z1 Pro League to directing cinematic campaigns for Fortnite and Call of Duty, I’ve spent my career turning competitive gaming moments into cultural touchstones. As a creative director and filmmaker, I’ve helmed 400+ esports broadcasts—reaching 200K+ live viewers and 100M+ impressions—while working with publishers like Epic Games, Xbox, and Riot. My hybrid background in UFC production and grassroots esports taught me one thing: authenticity always wins.

About Me:

At In Queue, I’m channeling that ethos into building tournaments that look as premium as they feel fair. Every observer angle, trailer, and graphic we create serves two masters: competitive clarity and community trust. (Yes, that means no scripted "storylines"—just raw, player-driven drama.) This is the project I’ve waited a decade for. Not just to make esports beautiful, but to prove indie production can outshine corporate budgets when you prioritize craft over clout.
