Mazer Gives Back is set to light up the Apex Legends scene tonight, April 16 at 7pm ET, and the anticipation is building. We’ve rounded up five teams whose recent form and distinctive stories make them must-watch contenders. Whether you’re a seasoned fan or a newcomer to competitive Apex, these teams are poised to create unforgettable moments on the battlefield.

5️⃣ Liquid Alienware: The Americas Heavyweight

It’s no shock to see Liquid Alienware on this list. Sitting 2nd in the Americas Pro League, they’re one of the biggest names in the scene.

Jaguares, Neazul, and Kurev lead this squad with top-tier mechanics and sharp teamplay. Kurev’s rise from a South American prodigy to a staple in one of esports’ premier orgs is a storyline worth following. Expect them to bring consistency and firepower into tonight’s matches.

4️⃣ Infinite: The Qualifier Champions

Infinite is the definition of earned success. After battling their way into Pro League through Preseason Qualifier 2, they immediately made noise by winning Day 1 of EMA Pro League.

With Kizaron, Vooshii, and Ye Qiu, this team thrives under pressure. Their adaptability and synergy have turned them into giant slayers. If they keep that up, they’ll be turning heads tonight.

3️⃣ Solis: The Sleeper Threat

Solis is a squad that thrives in the shadows of bigger names, but anyone paying attention knows they’ve been consistently going toe to toe with the top teams.

crestep, riley, and dog play with grit, resilience, and a touch of unpredictability that makes them one of the event’s biggest wildcards. If you’re rooting for an underdog (pun intended) to shake up the leaderboard, this is your squad.

2️⃣ HUSS: The Americas Rising Star

HUSS is on a serious hot streak, currently tied for 2nd in the Americas Pro League after a first-place finish in their opening week.

Led by dooplex, SkittleCakes, and gent, this team is fast, aggressive, and fearless. Their scrim results have been impressive, and the chemistry between these three is electric. Don’t be surprised if HUSS steals the show tonight.

1️⃣ NAVI: The EMEA Dominators

At the top of our list sits Natus Vincere (NAVI), and for good reason. They’re the most recent LAN Champions and the current #1 team in the EMEA Pro League, recently winning their last block by a staggering 50-point margin.

KiingZ, B1N, and WandzTV have formed an unshakable trio, mixing world-class mechanics with LAN-tested composure. No other team comes into Mazer Gives Back with NAVI’s level of dominance, and if they continue their reign, tonight’s event could be another chapter in their growing legacy.

With big names, breakout stories, and rising stars, Mazer Gives Back is shaping up to be a blast. And while we’ve spotlighted five of the top squads to watch, Apex is a game where anything can happen. Will one of these teams claim the crown, or will an underdog steal the spotlight?

Tune in tonight at 7PM ET to find out.
