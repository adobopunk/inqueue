In Queue is proud to power Queen’s Cup & Princess Cup Season 4 in partnership with Gaming Mpire and RynoLive. The competitive Apex Legends league will feature 40 teams across two divisions, battling over four weeks of league play, with placements determining each team’s division.

The season kicks off with placement matches on July 17 and 18 at 7:00 PM EST, hosted by WomensCircuits. Following the opening matches, teams will compete in weekly league matches, including Legends bans, rotating maps, and full stat tracking, culminating in the Match Point Finals of both Queen’s Cup and Princess Cup.

Participants will compete for a $1,000+ prize pool, with highlights and standings tracked throughout the season. Only one team will claim the crown as the ultimate champions of Season 4.
