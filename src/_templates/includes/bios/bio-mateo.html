Mateo Juliá Wise

He/him

Mateo’s creative journey began during his freshman year of college, when he was searching for connection in an otherwise isolating environment.

After some exploration, he joined a gaming club—and discovered firsthand the power of gaming to bring people together.

That experience sparked an interest in design, leading him to create posters to promote club events and help strengthen the community. By taping flyers around campus and seeing who showed up, Mateo got his first taste of event-focused graphic design.

Fast forward a few years, and after countless hours of gaming, designing, and experimenting, Mateo has turned his passion into a career. BoomTV gave him the opportunity to step into esports full-time, where he built premium graphics and event packages for brands like Intel, Xbox, Verizon, Activision, Samsung, Porsche, and Epic Games.

After three years at BoomTV (and seven-plus in the field), Mateo continues to push his creative skills to new heights.
