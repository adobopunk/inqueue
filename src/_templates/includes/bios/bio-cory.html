Cory Sobon

He/him

Cory’s passion for competitive gaming started with Call of Duty 2. Yes, that one. Whether it was grinding early GameBattles ladders or competing in events where thumbsticks were the grand prize, the thrill of competition was always top of mind.

So after a career of almost 10 years working long office hours in luxury auto sales, a change was needed and it was time to dive back into esports.

Starting with news coverage surrounding Gears of War esports, he earned an opportunity to attend PAX East 2016 as a media representative. There he was able to get a behind-the-scenes look at what went into the production and execution of events. There, he got a behind-the-scenes look at how events come together. As the battle royale genre exploded and titles like Apex Legends and Call of Duty: Warzone hit the scene, BoomTV brought Cory on as a full-time Project Manager.

During his time there, he worked with brands like Activision, the Army National Guard, EA, Mountain Dew Gaming, Xbox Game Pass, and other industry heavyweights—while also helping build spaces for both marginalized voices and up-and-coming players.

Now at In Queue, Cory is excited to keep building unforgettable experiences for players and viewers alike.
