Tifa Jade

She/her

TJ’s creative journey started with stop-motion action figure films as a kid, sparking a lifelong love of storytelling, motion, and the worlds we build around us. That path eventually led her from scrappy TV newsrooms to high-profile animation direction in esports—where she now brings cinematic flair to competitive gaming’s biggest stages.

After graduating from Eastern Washington University’s film program in 2016, she jumped into the media world as a low-paid, highly-stressed creative services director at a small NBC affiliate in her hometown.

Her first big break came when a self-produced high school football documentary landed her a role with the UFC, where she begain crafting athlete stories on the biggest stage in combat sports.

Her roots in competitive gaming date back to the H1Z1 Pro League, where she led creative development for Impact Gaming during the franchise’s brief run. She later joined the experiential marketing agency BoomTV as a Creative Producer, directing animation for marquee events and campaigns with Epic Games, Xbox Game Pass, and Activision.

TJ’s passion lies behind the scenes: building cinematic trailers and leveling up live broadcasts. She’s excited to bring that energy to In Queue, raising the bar for what esports can be.
