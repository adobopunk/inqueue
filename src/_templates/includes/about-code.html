Player Code of Conduct

Our Code of Conduct isn’t stuffed with confusing legal jargon. It’s built to reflect the kind of space we want to foster: fair, welcoming, and competitive. We also believe this kind of thing should grow with the community. We solicit player feedback after every event, and we read and consider all of it. Because that's how esports should be done: for the community.
