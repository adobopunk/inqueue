In Queue Community Code of Conduct

Last Updated: April 14, 2025

Welcome to In Queue, where competitive gaming thrives on fair play, mutual respect, and community spirit. This Code of Conduct outlines what we require from every participant and the standards we uphold to keep our events positive, inclusive, and competitive for everyone.

1. Community Commitment

By joining our tournaments, you become part of a community built on integrity and respect. We expect players to compete with honor, treat others (whether opponents, staff, or spectators) with kindness, and remember that how we play matters as much as winning. We welcome players from all backgrounds and strive to create a space where everyone feels safe and valued.

2. Fair Competition Standards

All participants must rely solely on their own skill and game knowledge gained through fair and permitted means. Cheating in any form is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Using unauthorized software, hardware modifications, or macros
  • Exploiting glitches or bugs to gain an unfair advantage
  • Match fixing, win trading, or intentionally manipulating results
  • Using opponent streams or broadcasts to gain information ("stream sniping")

If you see something suspicious, please report it to tournament staff immediately through the proper channels. Protecting match integrity is a shared responsibility.

3. Sportsmanship and Conduct

Professional, respectful behavior is expected at all times. Friendly competition and playful banter are part of gaming culture — personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language are not.

In Queue does not tolerate:

  • Hate speech or slurs of any kind
  • Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, or otherwise discriminatory remarks
  • Threats, bullying, or harassment — in-game, in chat, on and other external platforms, or on social media

We ask that players respect staff decisions, even in disagreement. Officials are here to maintain fair and fun experiences for everyone.

Team names, player usernames, logos, and sponsorships must remain appropriate for an all-inclusive, all-ages audience. Inappropriate or offensive branding may result in tournament disqualification.

4. Media and Content Rights

By participating in In Queue tournaments, you give permission for In Queue and its partners to broadcast, record, and share your gameplay, in-game name, and related content for event coverage and promotional purposes. These moments help grow our community and celebrate the players who make it special.

5. Dispute Resolution Process

If you believe a ruling was made in error, you can file an appeal by opening a help ticket in our Discord server within 48 hours of the decision. Be sure to include any relevant evidence, like screenshots, video clips, or match logs.

All appeals must be handled respectfully. Abusive or hostile communication towards staff during this process may lead to restrictions on your ability to appeal or participate in future events.

6. Participant Responsibilities

By entering any In Queue tournament, participants agree to read, understand, and follow the specific rules, formats, and requirements of that event. These details may vary between tournaments and are provided ahead of time through official event pages, Discord announcements, or match lobbies. Failure to follow individual tournament rules may result in disqualification or other penalties.

Participation in online tournaments comes with inherent risks. Internet connection issues, hardware malfunctions, or platform service interruptions can happen. While we work hard to run smooth, fair events, In Queue is not responsible for personal technical issues or third-party service failures.

We encourage all players to take care of their mental health and well-being throughout the competition. If you're feeling overwhelmed or need a break, please reach out to a tournament admin — we're here to support you.

Players under 18 who participate in an In Queue tournament represent they have obtained permission to compete from a parent or legal guardian, and that their parent or legal guardian has read and acknowledged this code of conduct on their behalf.

7. Enforcement and Consequences

Violating this Code of Conduct can result in penalties including warnings, disqualification, forfeiture of prizing, suspension from future events, or permanent bans — depending on the severity of the offense. All enforcement decisions are made by In Queue staff and are final.

8. Legal Framework

By participating in any In Queue tournament, participants agree to hold In Queue and its staff harmless from any claims or liabilities arising from their participation. Participants also waive the right to pursue claims through class action lawsuits or jury trials.

We may update these terms from time to time, with changes communicated through our official channels. We encourage the In Queue community to voice feedback in regards to this code of conduct, and promise to involve and include the community in revisions and changes.

Participant Pledge

By joining In Queue tournaments, players agree to uphold the following pledge:

"I confirm that I’ve read and understood this Code of Conduct. I commit to competing with integrity, treating others with respect, and upholding the fair play standards that make our events fun and welcoming. I understand that violations of these rules may affect my eligibility to compete in current and future tournaments."

Support & Reporting

To report conduct violations, technical issues, or request help, open a #help ticket in our Discord server. For general inquiries, email us at connect@playinqueue.com.
