Our Values

The four arrows in our logo stand for In Queue's four core values. Everything we build starts from them. They’re our promise to you, and what we expect in return when you compete, watch, or engage with our community.

Integrity Above All

  • We run every tournament transparently, from open rulebooks to partnerships that put the game first. We measure success by the moments players and fans remember.
  • Bring your best, respect the grind, and honor the fight. Whether you’re lifting a trophy or learning from a loss, how you play matters as much as how you win.

Community Is The Core

  • Every decision starts with a simple question: "What does the community actually want?" Tournaments are shaped by your input, feedback, and passion.
  • Respect the grind. Whether you’re a pro or a first-timer, we’re all here for the same love of the game.

Opportunity For Everyone

  • We're building competitions where background, connections and geography don't decide who gets a chance.
  • Whether you're a ranked player from a small town or a veteran from an overlooked region, your skill earns the spotlight here. Bring your best, and we'll handle the rest.

Excellence as Standard

  • We treat every detail like it's the grand finals.
  • Push greatness. If you talk trash, back it up. Respect the craft, respect each other.
