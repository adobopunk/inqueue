In Queue is an independent, full-service esports production and operations agency. We're proudly worker-owned and operated, founded by gaming events veterans who have worked across every level of the industry—from running local LANs to producing global championships.

We’re channeling our vast experience into building something bold and different, and we're committed to building a culture rooted in four key values:

  • Integrity.
  • Community.
  • Opportunity.
  • Excellence.

This is esports from a new perspective, one where the competitive spirit matters more than the bottom line. Every dollar we earn goes back into the community – funding better tournaments, fairer opportunities, and the authentic esports experience fans deserve. No outside investors. No profit-above-all mentality. No compromises. Just the pure love of the game.

We believe the strongest partnerships are built on shared values. If you're as committed as we are to making competition more authentic, fair, and thrilling for players and fans alike, we’d love to collaborate.

Who We Are

We’re the disillusioned architects of esports’ biggest stages – now building something that we're deeply passionate about.

Directing all things operational is Cory Sobon, a veteran tournament director who's administered over 300 events and paid out more than $3 million in prizes. Having worked with giants like Activision, Respawn, and Xbox while founding the community-focused Proving Grounds circuit, Cory, known in the gaming sphere as Mazida, brings both elite experience and a refusal to compromise competitive integrity.

Our creative vision comes from Tiny Pond - the powerhouse creative studio run by partners Mateo Julia-Wise and Tifa Jade (TJ). Mateo brings six years of esports design mastery, from grassroots tournaments to events for Tier 2 Overwatch, Fortnite, and Rocket League.

TJ merges UFC production pedigree with deep esports roots that date back to the H1Z1 Pro League. She's directed cinematic event visuals for some of the biggest tournaments and influencers in the gaming sphere. Together, their work has ignited hype for over 400 tournaments, commanding main stages with 200K+ live viewers and amassing 100M+ impressions.

In Queue's Pillars

Our logo represents the four core values at In Queue. These are foundation of everything we build here. They’re our promise to you, and what we expect in return when you compete, watch, or engage with our community.

Integrity Above All

  • We're passionate about protecting the soul of competition. Every tournament is built on radical transparency, from open rulebooks to partnerships that put the game first. We measure success in unforgettable moments, not profit margins.
  • Bring your best, respect the grind, and honor the fight. Whether you’re lifting a trophy or learning from a loss, how you play matters as much as how you win. Together, we keep esports real.

Community Is The Core

  • Every decision starts with a simple question: "What does the community actually want?" Tournaments are shaped by your input, feedback, and passion.
  • Respect the grind. Whether you’re a pro or a first-timer, we’re all here for the same love of the game.

Opportunity For Everyone

  • We're building an esports ecosystem where background, connections, and geography don't dictate opportunity - because greatness can come from anywhere.
  • Whether you're a ranked warrior grinding from a small town or a veteran player from an overlooked region, your skill earns the spotlight here. Bring your best, and we'll handle the rest - from fair brackets to global visibility.

Excellence as Standard

  • We don’t do ‘good enough.’ Neither should you. We promise to treat every detail like it’s the grand finals. Premium experiences, no compromises.
  • Push greatness. If you talk trash—back it up. Respect the craft, respect each other.
