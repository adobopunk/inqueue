What's In Queue?

We're a worker-owned, full-service events production and operations agency.

In Queue was founded by the team that designed and produced events like The Last Dance in Verdansk, Code Freedom, Caldera Challenge, and Tfue Tuesday.

At In Queue, we hold ourselves to four commitments: integrity and transparency, community-driven projects, opportunity for everyone, and operational excellence. Ownership and decision-making are shared equally. Every team member is an owner, and we make decisions against the company values we set together.

If you're a brand, team or community leader looking for experienced event producers, influencer marketing and creative work, connect with us. We can plan and run your next event and help you reach the gaming community.
