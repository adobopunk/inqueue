What's In Queue?

We're a worker-owned, full-service esports production and operations agency.

In Queue was created by the former BoomTV talent behind standout events like The Last Dance in Verdansk, Code Freedom, Caldera Challenge, and Tfue Tuesday.

Though our roots trace back to the cutting-edge production and design we established at BoomTV, we're setting ourselves apart with a commitment to upholding the highest levels of integrity, championing community-driven projects, building opportunities for everyone, and maintaining operational excellence. (See: Our Values)

Our operating model dictates that ownership, voice, and vision are shared equally. Every team member is an owner, and every decision reflects our commitment to those company values we set together.

We created In Queue because the next generation of esports deserves an agency run by the people who do the work. We embrace creative risks, maintain collective ownership of the brand, and define our success by our impact on the community, not profits.

If you’re a brand, team, or community leader seeking an agency with veteran gaming events expertise and a human-first perspective, connect with us! We’re ready to elevate your next event and help you connect authentically with the gaming community.
