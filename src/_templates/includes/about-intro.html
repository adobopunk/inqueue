What's In Queue?

We're a worker-owned, full-service events production and operations agency.

In Queue was founded by the talent that brought cutting-edge design, behind standout events like The Last Dance in Verdansk, Code Freedom, Caldera Challenge, and Tfue Tuesday.

With In Queue, we've set ourselves apart from our past with a firm commitment to upholding the highest levels of integrity and transparency, championing community-driven projects, building opportunities for everyone, and maintaining operational excellence. Our operating model dictates that ownership, voice, and vision are shared equally. Every team member is an owner, and every decision reflects our commitment to those company values we set together.

If you’re a brand, team, or community leader seeking veteran events pedigree, influencer marketing expertise, and top-tier creative talent with a human-first perspective, connect with us! We’re ready to elevate your next event and help you connect authentically with the gaming community.
