Meet The Team

Cory Sobon

Cory’s passion for competitive gaming began back in Call of Duty 2. Yes, that one. Whether it was playing in the very early days of Gamebattles or playing in events with thumbsticks as prizes, competition was always top of his mind. Having to be responsible, he began his career in luxury automotive sales where his love for all things cars could flourish. After almost 10 years of long office hours, a change was needed and it was time to dive back into esports.

Tifa Jade

TJ’s creative journey started with stop-motion action figure films as a kid, sparking a lifelong love of storytelling, motion, and the worlds we build around us. That path eventually led her from scrappy TV newsrooms to high-profile animation direction in esports—where she now brings cinematic flair to competitive gaming’s biggest stages.

Mateo Juliá Wise

Mateo’s creative journey began during his freshman year of college, when he was searching for connection in an otherwise isolating environment. After some exploration, he joined a gaming club—and discovered firsthand the power of gaming to bring people together.
