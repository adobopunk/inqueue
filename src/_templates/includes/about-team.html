Meet The Team

Cory Sobon

Cory’s passion for competitive gaming began back in Call of Duty 2. Yes, that one. Whether it was playing in the very early days of Gamebattles or playing in events with thumbsticks as prizes, competition was always top of his mind. He started his career in luxury car sales, where he could spend his days around cars. After almost 10 years of long office hours, he went back to esports.

Tifa Jade

TJ started making stop-motion action figure films as a kid, which turned into a lifelong love of storytelling and motion. That led her from small TV newsrooms to animation direction in esports, where she now directs animation for some of competitive gaming's biggest events.

Mateo Juliá Wise

Mateo’s creative journey began during his freshman year of college, when he was searching for connection in an otherwise isolating environment. After some exploration, he joined a gaming club—and discovered firsthand the power of gaming to bring people together.
